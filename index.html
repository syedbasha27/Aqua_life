Disease Guide

Spot It. Treat It. Fast.

Common pond diseases and the right product to use — from a farmer's field guide.

⚪

White Spot Syndrome (WSSV)

Most destructive viral disease in vannamei prawns. White spots on shell, lethargy, mass mortality.

✅ Use ViraKill BKC 80%
🟢

Green Algae Bloom

Oxygen crash in ponds due to algae overgrowth. Causes early morning mortality and off-flavour in shrimp.

✅ Use ClearPond Zeolite
💀

Early Mortality Syndrome (EMS)

Bacterial infection causing mass shrimp death within first 35 days. Pale hepatopancreas, abnormal behaviour.

✅ Use PondPro + ViraKill
🔴

Red Leg Disease

Bacterial hemorrhage causing reddening of legs and swimmerets. Seen in Rohu, Catla, and Tilapia fish.

✅ Use ParaKill DDVP
🍂

Black Gill Disease

Caused by filamentous bacteria and poor water quality. Darkened gills, reduced feeding, weight loss.

✅ Use PondPro Bacillus
💪

Low Immunity / Stress

After transport, temperature change or water quality drop. Shrimp stop feeding, become lethargic.

✅ Use ShrimpVit Booster
